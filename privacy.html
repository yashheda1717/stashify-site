Privacy Policy

How Stashify handles your information

PrivacyTerms of UseSupportLicences

The short version

  • Your documents stay on your iPhone. Stashify has no accounts and no servers. We never receive, see or store your documents, photos, text or settings.
  • No tracking, no ads, no analytics. Stashify contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code, and does not share data with data brokers.
  • Reading and AI happen on the device. Text recognition uses Apple’s Vision framework, and AI features use Apple Intelligence’s on-device model. Nothing is sent to us or to any AI company.
  • You are in control. Delete any document, erase a profile, or delete the app to remove everything it stored.

Effective 24 September 2026. This policy covers the Stashify app for iPhone and its widgets.

1. Who we are

Stashify is published by Tekhnologia Innovations (“we”, “us”), based in Maharashtra, India. For any question about privacy, write to tekhnologiaindia@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. Stashify does not require an account, does not ask for your name or contact details for our use, and does not transmit your content to us or to any third party. We have no server that stores user data.

In the words of Apple’s App Store privacy labels: Data Not Collected.

3. Information stored on your device

To work, Stashify keeps the following only on your iPhone, inside the app’s private storage:

  • The documents, photos, scans and files you add, and text read from them so you can search inside them.
  • Your sections, subsections, profile names, file title, notes, signatures you place, reminders and app settings.
  • Small preview images (thumbnails) of your documents.
  • If you set a DigiLocker PIN, a salted one-way hash of it (never the PIN itself).

This information is protected by iOS data protection and is included in your own iPhone backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) if you have backups turned on, under Apple’s terms. We cannot access your backups.

4. Permissions and why we ask

PermissionUsed for
CameraScanning documents with Apple’s document camera. Images stay on your device.
PhotosChoosing photos or screenshots you want to file, and saving an exported file to Photos if you choose to. Stashify only sees the items you pick.
Face IDUnlocking DigiLocker and, if you turn it on, the app. Face ID is handled entirely by iOS; Stashify never receives your face data.

Every permission is optional. You can change them at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

5. On-device processing and AI

Stashify reads text in photos and scans with Apple’s Vision framework, and uses Apple Intelligence’s on-device language model (on supported iPhones running iOS 26 or later) to suggest sections, file documents, write titles and summaries, and sort a scanned pile of paper. This processing runs on your iPhone. On iPhones without Apple Intelligence, Stashify uses built-in rules instead. We do not send your documents or their text to any AI service.

6. Purchases

Stashify Plus is sold through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your payment details, name or Apple Account information. The app checks with Apple, on your device, whether Plus is active. Apple’s handling of purchases is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

7. Widgets, Siri and Spotlight

If you add Stashify widgets, the app saves a small summary on your device for them to display: your file title, document counts, and the titles and previews of recent and pinned documents. DigiLocker documents are never included, not even their names. This summary never leaves your device.

8. Sharing and exporting

Documents leave Stashify only when you choose to export or share them. You decide where they go (for example Mail, Messages, AirDrop or Files), and the receiving app’s or person’s privacy practices then apply.

9. Keeping and deleting your information

  • Deleting a document moves it to Recently Deleted, where it is kept for 30 days so you can recover it, then permanently shredded. You can shred it sooner.
  • Archived documents stay on your device until you restore or delete them.
  • “Erase this profile” in Settings › Data permanently removes that profile’s documents and sections.
  • Deleting the Stashify app removes everything it stored on your iPhone.

10. Security

Your files are stored in the app’s sandbox with iOS data protection. DigiLocker opens only with Face ID or your PIN and locks itself automatically. Because nothing is stored on our side, there is no account of yours for anyone to breach.

11. Children

Stashify is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the EU and UK GDPR and US state privacy laws give you rights to access, correct and delete personal data. Because we do not collect or hold your personal data, the data Stashify uses is already in your hands, and you can view, change, export or delete it inside the app. If you have a question or complaint, contact us at tekhnologiaindia@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date. If a change means the app would begin collecting data, we will tell you in the app first.
