Frequently asked questions

Where are my documents stored?

Only on your iPhone, inside Stashify. We have no servers and never receive your documents. They are included in your iPhone backup if backups are on.

How do I restore Stashify Plus on a new iPhone?

Open Stashify, tap Profile › Stashify Plus › Restore purchases, while signed in with the Apple Account you bought Plus with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In Stashify go to Settings › Plus › Manage subscription, or in the iPhone Settings app tap your name › Subscriptions › Stashify. You keep Plus until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I forgot my DigiLocker PIN.

For your security the PIN is never stored, only a one-way hash, so it cannot be recovered. If Face ID for DigiLocker is on, you can still open it with Face ID and set a new PIN in Settings › Security.

I deleted a document by mistake.

Open Search (or Settings › Data) and tap Recently deleted. Documents are kept there for 30 days before they are shredded for good.

How does Declutter work?

With Stashify Plus, scan up to 100 pages in one go. Stashify reads each page on your iPhone, groups pages that belong together, and suggests a section and subsection for each document. You review everything before it is filed.

Why is AI sorting not available on my iPhone?

AI features use Apple Intelligence, which needs a supported iPhone on iOS 26 or later with Apple Intelligence turned on. Without it, Stashify sorts using the words it reads on each page.

How do I switch to the classic wooden wheel?

With Plus, go to Settings › Plus and turn on Classic mode.
